## 2. Core feeling
"soft plead to stay, before becoming enraged with violent desire". The softness is the resting state and the rage is the interruption: it breaks through in the screams and the song falls straight back into the plead. *(Claude's reading, which Kiwi confirmed: every return to soft is sadder than the last, and the song ends where it began, worn down.)*

## 3. What the world is made of
"nerves or a nervous-system like of oscillating, palpitating axons". *(Claude had proposed: a network that grows across everything, delicate and caring at first and looking like a net or wiring by the end, with small points of light moving through it, the "eyes". Kiwi: "Yes to 1" and the nervous-system wording above.)*

## 4. How things move *(proposed by Claude, accepted by Kiwi: "Yes to 2")*
- Verse 1 (soft, devoted): reaches, offers.
- The screams: split, tear.
- The melancholic pleads: sink, tremble.
- Verse 2 (watching): hover, cling, follow.
- Outro: thins, fades.

## 5. Light and temperature *(proposed by Claude, accepted by Kiwi: "Yes to 3")*
A dark base, cold and quiet during the soft parts, white and pale for the "eyes". Each scream is a sudden hot flare (red or gold) that dies back to cold.

## 6. The arc *(proposed by Claude, accepted by Kiwi: "Yes to 4")*
Four states: (1) plead (devotion) in verse 1; (2) the rupture and the retreat in the chorus; (3) the watching in verse 2; (4) the return and fade in chorus 2 and the outro. Transitions are slow melts everywhere except the screams, which are hard cuts that melt back.

## 7. Must never look like
"It cannot read as a fancy geometric screensaver. Nothing literal in terms of shape or perceived motion." *(Also accepted from Claude's defaults, "Yes to 5": never rainbow, never cute or romantic, no literal eyes or faces.)*

## 8. Relationship to the song *(proposed by Claude, accepted by Kiwi: "Yes to 6")*
It mirrors the plead on the surface and lets the violence show underneath.

## 9. Per scene notes *(proposed by Claude, accepted by Kiwi: "Yes to 7")*
- The screams are the click accents (four in all).
- Pen: wake in verse 1, chased or pulled toward you in the chorus, push away in verse 2.

On how the scenes should be made: "What each verse/chorus says would make more sense in how the scenes look. How to represent the core feelings of the singer's state at that point in the song using motion and visuals is the correct bet rather than doing different tiers of softness as an overarching concept." And on the song: "its a song you sit with and ponder with the lyrics, almost as if being handheld by the singer across her mind state."
