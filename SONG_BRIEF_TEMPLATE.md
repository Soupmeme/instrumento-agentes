# SONG_BRIEF (Descripción de la canción)

Copy this file to `SONG_BRIEF.md` and fill it in your own words (English or Spanish, either is fine). I will not rewrite your wording: everything I derive goes into DECISIONS.md, EXPLAINER.md and `scenes.json`, with a pointer back to the line of your brief that justified it. Rough answers are enough. Items 1, 2, 4, 6 and 7 are required; the rest are strongly wanted, and where you leave them out I will propose a default and ask you to confirm it.

Short is fine, but "behavior words" matter more than "feeling words": "melancholic" alone gives me nothing to build, "slow, sparse, things drift apart and never meet" does.

## 1. The song (La canción) *required*
- Title, artist, duration:
- Section map with rough timestamps (your own names are fine: intro, verse, chorus, bridge, drop, outro, "the part where the voice breaks"):

## 2. Core feeling, as a tension between two things (Sentimiento central) *required*
For example "calm on the surface, pressure underneath", "joy that is slightly desperate".

## 3. What the world is made of (Metáfora material)
Smoke, mycelium, a swarm of insects, ink in water, neon rain, a tide, something else?

## 4. How things move (Verbos de movimiento) *required*
Drift, cling, scatter, converge, pulse, tear, settle... per section if it changes. How fast, how dense?

## 5. Light and temperature (Luz y temperatura)
Dark or bright base, warm or cold, high or low contrast, glow or grain or flat.

## 6. The arc (El arco) *required*
Three to six named states from start to end, and how you get from each to the next (slow melt, hard cut, slow build then release).

## 7. Must never look like (Nunca debe verse como) *required*
One or two explicit anti-references ("never a screensaver", "never rainbow", "never cute").

## 8. Relationship to the song (Relación con la canción)
Does it illustrate the lyrics, mirror the mood, contradict it on purpose, or hold space while the song leads?

## 9. Notes per scene, in your words (Notas por escena)
Which moments deserve an accent (a click)? Where should the pen mean something specific (push, pull, calm, wake)?

## 10. Performance intent (Intención al tocar)
Which moments do you want to be able to push with the wheel, and which do you want to leave alone?

---

How many scenes will this make? The instrument works best with about 3 to 8 scenes of at least 15 seconds each. If your arc has more states than that, say which ones could be the same scene at a different wheel level.
