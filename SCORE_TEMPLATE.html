Visual score (Partitura visual)

A blank score, to fill by hand or on screen. It is a list of scenes and gestures, never of keystrokes or parameters, and it guides the performance without automating it. Nothing here is content: the song, the passages and the choices are yours.

Before the performance (Antes de la presentación)

  • ☐ The scene table matches scenes.json (open the cue panel, key C, and read the list).
  • ☐ The song plays from the same file or link used in front of the class.
  • ☐ I have walked the whole score at least once with the real song.
  • ☐ I know where S (safe mode) is, in case the picture stutters.
  • ☐ The help overlay (key H) is closed.
